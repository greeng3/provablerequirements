When a requirement's stored verdict holds on both a mechanical basis, established by a harness provreq generated from the formal claim, and an asserted basis no stronger than it, established by a human-tagged test provreq merely ran and passed, the traceability report recommends retiring that tagged test, naming its source location. The recommendation is advice, never an action: provreq flags the redundancy and the operator decides, and provreq never retires anything on its own. Each recommendation carries the caveat that the mechanical verdict establishes only the formal claim, which is often narrower than the requirement's prose, so the report never asserts the two check the same behaviour. A tagged test whose verdict is stronger than, or incomparable to, any mechanical result is never recommended for retirement.
