Not every recorded claim about a verdict can be checked. Where a verdict carries the environment
it was produced in, that environment can be compared against the current one and confirmed
unchanged. Where a verdict carries no such record — because it predates the tool recording one —
there is nothing to compare, and the verdict is correctly not reported as drifted.

These two situations must not look alike to an operator. A verdict whose environment was recorded
and still matches carries a checked claim; a verdict with no record carries an unchecked one, and
both are equally free of drift. Presenting them identically lets the stronger reading win by
default, so the operator infers a guarantee the record does not carry.

Wherever a stored verdict is presented, the surface therefore states where that verdict was
produced, or states plainly that this was never recorded, and names re-verifying as the way to
record it. This changes what the operator is told, never whether a verdict counts as drifted: an
absent record must not be turned into a drift it cannot substantiate.
