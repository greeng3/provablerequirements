The "Create sample content" choice during onboarding (per
UX-postInitChoice) shall generate a small demonstration set of
Collections and artifacts within the new Project — for example,
a requirements Collection with two or three sample requirements
linked to one another with typed links. The sample content is
intended for orientation and is immediately editable or
deletable by the user; it is never auto-regenerated or
protected.
