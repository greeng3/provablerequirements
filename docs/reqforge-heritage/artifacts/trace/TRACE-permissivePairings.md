Any link type may relate any two artifacts regardless of their
respective Collections or artifact types. ReqForge shall not
enforce type-pair restrictions; for example, verifies is not
restricted to test-to-requirement pairings, and satisfies is not
restricted to design-to-requirement pairings. This permissiveness
is deliberate and aligned with doorstop's posture, on the
understanding that engineering teams use traceability in ways
that rigid type systems often fail to anticipate.
