ReqForge shall support three shapes of artifact: content-hosted
(content authored in and managed by ReqForge), uploaded-blob (binary
files stored in the project tree), and URL-reference (pointers to
external content viewable via a browser). All three shapes shall
participate uniformly in the traceability graph.
